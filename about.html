Instant-print photobooth in Hanoi

Happy Booth rents out instant-print photobooths for weddings, birthdays and company events in Hanoi. Our job is simple: set up a great-looking photo corner, let guests shoot as much as they like, and make sure everyone leaves with a print.

Every rental comes with two staff who set up, guide guests and look after the booth all night. Each party gets its own frame with the host's names and date, and after the party you receive all the digital files.
